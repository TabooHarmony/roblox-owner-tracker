roblox-owner-tracker

Experimental owner-count estimates via rank-neighbor comparison. Method by Maggy (Maggy Rarefication); this is a deterministic reimplementation.

Experimental — uncalibrated. These are not owner counts and not confidence intervals. Each result is a comparison against neighboring items in an all-time best-selling ranking, built from scraped wiki evidence. Wide brackets mean weak evidence, not precision. Bundles are out of scope.
